# Premium Avatar Assets

These optimized WebP files are local copies of the six avatar images that were
already part of the project and hosted under its Base44 media account. They were
resized from 1024x1024 PNG to 512x512 WebP for faster loading and offline use.

No third-party character pack was added here. Large VRoid and sprite packs found
during the performance pass were intentionally excluded because they would add
roughly 13-49 MB to the application.
